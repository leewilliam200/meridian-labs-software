Delete your Summit account

You can delete your Summit account and everything in it at any time.

In the app

Open Summit, go to Settings and tap Delete Account. Confirm, and your account and data are deleted straight away.

Can't open the app?

Email support.summit.app@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". We'll delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

Before you delete

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions) or on iPhone in Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions.

What gets deleted

Your account (email and name), your streak and relapse history, urge logs and triggers, goals, your Why, and your reading history.

What we keep

Purchase records held by Google Play, Apple and our payments provider RevenueCat, which they keep for tax and fraud prevention. App usage events that were linked to your account are kept without your account on them, so they can't be traced back to you.
