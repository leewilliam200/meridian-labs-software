What you can post

You can add meals, prices and conditions, photos, short notes, confirmations that a listing is still right, corrections, and reports about a listing that looks wrong or has closed. Receipts or other evidence you send so we can check something stay private and are only seen by us.

Rules for posting

  • Keep it true and first-hand. Post what you saw or paid yourself, with the date you saw it. Don't guess or make things up, and don't post fake confirmations or reports to push a place up or down.
  • Use your own photos. Only upload photos you took yourself or have the right to share. No photos copied from the web, a venue's page or another person.
  • Leave people out. No identifiable faces, and no names, phone numbers, email addresses, card details or receipts showing personal information. Notes are shown publicly with the listing.
  • Keep it decent. Nothing offensive, harassing, hateful, sexual, misleading or against the law.
  • No spam or hidden advertising. If you own or work for a venue, or are paid by it, say so in your note, and don't post just to promote it.
