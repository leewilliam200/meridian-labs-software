BrokeMap

Cheap meals near you in Sydney.

BrokeMap answers one question: what can I eat near me for under $X right now? It shows meals around Sydney that cost A$18 or less, on a map, with the price you actually pay, any conditions (student card, lunch only, a drink you have to buy) and whether the place is open now.

Prices come from our own checks and from people who ate there. Each listing says where its facts came from and how recently someone confirmed them, so you can tell a fresh tip from an old one.

Where it's at

BrokeMap is in development and isn't open to the public yet. We're starting with Sydney.

Accounts

You can browse without an account. You only need one to save meals or to add and correct listings. You sign in with a code sent to your email, or with Google. There's no public profile, and your name is never shown next to anything you add.
