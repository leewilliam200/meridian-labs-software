Easeoff

Quit vaping and nicotine pouches, one step at a time.

Easeoff is an iPhone and Android app that helps you cut down or stop vapes and nicotine pouches. It builds a plan from how much you use now, gets you through cravings with quick techniques, and shows what you've saved.
