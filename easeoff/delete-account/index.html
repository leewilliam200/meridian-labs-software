Delete your Easeoff account

You can delete your Easeoff account and everything in it at any time.

In the app

Open Easeoff, go to the Plan tab, open Settings and tap Delete account. You can also find Delete account at the bottom of the subscription screen. Confirm, and your account and data are deleted straight away.

Can't open the app?

Email admin@meridianlabssoftware.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Easeoff account". We'll delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

Before you delete

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription. Cancel it first: on iPhone in Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions; on Android in Google Play, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions.

What gets deleted

Your account, email address and any name or picture from Apple or Google, your setup answers, your plan and stop date, everything you logged (uses, cravings, places and triggers), your milestones and the prices you entered.

What we keep

Purchase records held by Apple, Google and our subscriptions provider RevenueCat, which they keep for tax and fraud prevention. App usage events that were linked to your account are kept with your account removed from them, so they can't be traced back to you.
