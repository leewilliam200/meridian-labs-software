Easeoff privacy policy

What Easeoff collects, why, who else handles it, and how to delete it.

Last updated 25 November 2026

Easeoff is an app that helps you cut down or stop vaping and nicotine pouches. It is made by Meridian Labs, a small software business in Australia ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Easeoff app on iPhone and Android.

The short version

  • We collect your email address (plus your name and picture if Apple or Google share them), your setup answers and plan, and what you log in the app.
  • We use it to run the app for you and to learn which parts of the app help people.
  • No ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, and we never sell your data.
  • You can delete your account and data in the app at any time.

What we collect

Your account

Your email address. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we get what they share with us: your email address (Apple can give us a private relay address instead of your real one) and, if they include them, your name and profile picture. The app doesn't show or use your name or picture. If you use a password, it is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form, so we can't read it.

Your setup answers and plan

What you use (vapes, pouches or both), how much and how strong, what it costs you, whether you use nicotine replacement, your reasons for cutting down (including any you type yourself), the plan you choose, your stop date, and how strong your urge was before and after the first technique you try.

What you log

Each use or slip you log (when, how strong, and where you were if you add it), each craving (how strong, which techniques you tried, what set it off and how it ended), the milestones you reach and any price changes you enter.

How the app is used

Simple usage events, such as reaching a setup step, finishing a craving or seeing the subscription screen. Each one carries your account id, a random install id the app makes for itself, your phone's platform (iOS or Android) and the app version. We don't use advertising ids.

Subscriptions

Payments are handled by Apple or Google. Our subscriptions provider RevenueCat tells the app whether your subscription or free trial is active, linked to your account id. We never see your card details.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, Sentry receives a report: what went wrong in the app and device details such as your phone model, system version, language and time zone. A report also carries an anonymous id for this install of the app and the approximate city it came from, which Sentry works out from the connection. Crash reports are not linked to your account, and Sentry is set up not to store IP addresses.

Reminders

Reminders, such as the one before your free trial ends, are scheduled on your phone itself. They are not sent from our servers.

Health information

What you enter about your nicotine use can count as health information in some places. We only have it because you entered it, and we only use it to run your plan in the app.

Why we use it

  • To run your account, keep your plan and logs, and show your progress on any phone you sign in on.
  • To check whether your trial or subscription is active.
  • To find and fix crashes.
  • To see, across everyone, which parts of the app help and where people get stuck, so we can improve it.
  • To answer you when you email us.

We don't use your data for advertising and we don't sell it or share it for advertising.

Who else handles it

These companies run parts of the app for us. They get only what they need to do that job.

  • Supabase: our database and sign-in. Your account and everything you enter is stored with Supabase on servers in the United States.
  • RevenueCat: keeps track of subscriptions and free trials (United States).
  • Apple and Google: take payments, and run Sign in with Apple and Google sign-in when you choose them.
  • Sentry: crash reports (United States).
  • Resend: sends our emails, such as a password reset code (United States).

We may also share information if the law requires it.

Because these providers store data in the United States, your information is held outside Australia. We choose providers that protect it with security measures at least as strong as we would use ourselves.

How we keep it safe

Data travels between the app and our servers encrypted. Our database only lets each account read and change its own rows. No system is completely secure, so we keep what we collect to the minimum the app needs.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. When you delete your account, your account and everything you entered are deleted straight away. Usage events are kept with your account removed from them. Crash reports are deleted by Sentry automatically after a limited time. Apple, Google and RevenueCat keep purchase records under their own policies, for tax and fraud prevention.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete: in the app, open the Plan tab, then Settings, then Delete account. If you can't open the app, see Delete your Easeoff account.
  • See, correct or get a copy: email us and we'll reply within 30 days. You can change your plan and the prices you pay in the app at any time.
  • Reminders: turn them off in your phone's notification settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have more rights under local law, such as the right to object or to complain to a privacy regulator. In Australia that is the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au). Please contact us first so we can try to fix it.

Age

Easeoff is for adults aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think someone under 18 has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use the data you've already given us, we'll email you before it applies.
