Job Pipeline privacy policy

Last updated 3 October 2026.

This policy covers Job Pipeline, an add-on for ServiceM8 made by Meridian Labs (ABN 88 792 692 097), an Australian sole trader. "We" means Meridian Labs. "You" means the ServiceM8 account that connects the add-on and the staff who use it. It sits alongside ServiceM8's own privacy policy, which covers everything ServiceM8 does with your account.

The short version

  • Job Pipeline reads job, schedule, client name, staff name and job category data from your ServiceM8 account, only to draw the board you're looking at.
  • It never writes to your account, and it doesn't keep a copy of your data anywhere.
  • We don't sell, share or analyse your data, and we don't use it to train AI.

What the add-on reads

When someone opens the board or presses Refresh, ServiceM8 runs the add-on on its own hosting and gives it a short-lived, read-only access token for your account. With it, the add-on reads:

  • Jobs: job number, status, job address, description, value, and the dates for quoting, work order, completion, invoice sent and payment received.
  • Schedule: booking and check-in times and which staff member each belongs to.
  • Clients: client names only, matched to the jobs on the board.
  • Staff: first and last names.
  • Job categories: names and colours.

It doesn't read client contact details, phone numbers, email addresses, notes, photos, attachments, payment details or card data.

Where it goes and how long we keep it

The add-on runs as a ServiceM8-hosted function. The data it reads is processed in memory, sent to your browser or the ServiceM8 app to draw the board, and discarded when the request ends. We don't run our own servers or database for Job Pipeline, so there is nowhere for us to keep a copy.

The function writes short technical log lines (your ServiceM8 account ID, how many jobs were loaded, how long it took, and error messages). These contain no client, staff or job details. ServiceM8's hosting holds them, and we read them only to fix problems.

Nothing is saved in your browser beyond normal page caching, and the board uses no cookies or tracking.

What we collect directly

If you email us for support, we keep your email and whatever you choose to include, so we can answer and keep a record of the conversation. We use it for nothing else. Please don't send client details or passwords; a job number is enough.

If you pay for the add-on through ServiceM8's billing, ServiceM8 handles the payment. We see the account name and amount in ServiceM8's payout reports, and never see card details.

Who we share it with

Nobody, except where the law requires it. ServiceM8 provides the hosting and billing that the add-on runs on.

Your rights

You can disconnect the add-on at any time from Features & Integrations in ServiceM8. That stops all access straight away; there is no stored data to delete. You can ask what support emails we hold about you, ask us to correct them, or ask us to delete them, by writing to the address below. We handle personal information under the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). If you're unhappy with how we've handled a request, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Security

Access tokens are issued by ServiceM8 for each request, expire within 15 minutes, are read-only, and are never logged or stored by us.

Changes

If the add-on starts reading more data or keeping anything, we'll update this page and the date at the top before that change goes live.
